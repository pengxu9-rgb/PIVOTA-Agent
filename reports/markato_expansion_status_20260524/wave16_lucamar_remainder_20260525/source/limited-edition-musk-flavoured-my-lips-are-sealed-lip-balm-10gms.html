NATURAL & ORGANIC LIP BALM 10g

 

Unveil the timeless secret to soft, supple lips with this vintage inspired Lanolin Lip Balm.

Infused with pure lanolin, this rich emollient lip balm deeply nourishes & protects, soothing dry chapped lips.

Its silky smooth texture glides on effortlessly, forming a protective barrier that locks in moisture and restores your lips natural softness.

Treat your lips to the nourishment they deserve.

 

Lanolin used in all our products  is humanely sourced from Australian sheep farmers.

Our Lip Balm is cruelty free and never tested on animals.
