Got questions? We've got answers

Our team of experts has the answers.

Is this product safe to use?

Our product is produced following strict standards and holds certifications such as ISO and GMP, ensuring its safety and quality.
Does this serum suit my skin?

If you're uncertain about whether this product aligns with your skin's needs, please take our skin type quiz. This will help you determine your skin type and offer customised product recommendations. Prioritising your skin's well-being, we aim to guide you towards the best choices.
How should I include this serum in my skincare routine?

Cleanse, tone (if using a toner), and wait until it dries before applying the serum. Afterwards, apply your moisturiser and, during the day, your sunscreen. We advise using the serum both AM and PM.

Can this serum cause irritation?

The Hyaluronic Acid Serum has been formulated with sensitive skin in mind. It contains B12 and B5, ingredients reducing redness and inflammation caused by skin sensitivity. However, skin reactions vary among individuals. If irritation does occur, please consult a specialist.
When can I expect to see results using this serum?

Results differ per individual, but many notice immediate comfort and hydrated skin. For optimal outcomes, regularly integrate the serum into your skincare routine.
Can this serum be used in combination with other serums?

Yes, especially if addressing multiple skin concerns. However, we strongly suggest taking our quiz for a customised skincare routine. If you have certain skin conditions, consult a specialist before combining serums.
Can this serum be used on its own, or should it be used with a moisturiser?

We recommend complementing it with a moisturiser, particularly for sensitive skin. This not only seals in the benefits of the serum but also offers added hydration and protection, optimising your skincare routine. 
What's the shelf life of the serum after opening?

For optimal efficacy, use the serum within 12 months of opening. Store away from direct sunlight and extreme temperatures to maintain its quality.
