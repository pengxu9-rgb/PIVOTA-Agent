## What this app is

Pivota is a **read-only** ChatGPT app. It lets you search a normalized catalog of beauty and skincare
products and retrieve evidence-graded product and ingredient information with citations. It does **not** create
an account, sign you in, take payment, or place orders.

## Data we process

The Pivota app connects to our server anonymously. When you ask ChatGPT something that uses a Pivota tool, the
app sends us **only** the specific inputs needed to answer:

- **Search and lookup inputs** — the search terms, filters (category, price range), and product identifiers
  that your request requires. These are the parameters ChatGPT passes to the tool for that single request.
- **Network metadata** — your IP address is visible to our servers at the moment of the request and is used
  transiently for security and rate-limiting (preventing abuse of the public endpoint).

We do **not** collect, request, or store:

- account credentials, passwords, or authentication tokens (the app requires none);
- payment-card information, financial account details, or government identifiers;
- health information;
- your precise location;
- your ChatGPT conversation history or any content beyond the specific tool inputs above.

## How we use it

We use the tool inputs **only** to generate and return the requested product results, and we use network
metadata **only** to operate and protect the service (rate-limiting, abuse and fraud prevention, debugging).
We do not build advertising or behavioral profiles, and we do not use your inputs to track you across
sessions or services.

## Retention

Operational request logs (including the tool inputs and the requesting IP) are retained for up to
**[e.g. 30] days** for security and reliability, then deleted or aggregated into non-identifying statistics.
We do not maintain a per-user profile because the app has no accounts.

## Sharing

We do **not** sell your data. The product and ingredient information returned by the app is Pivota's own
catalog and licensed/first-party data. We share the limited data above only with infrastructure providers
that host and operate the service on our behalf (e.g. cloud hosting), under confidentiality obligations, and
only as needed to run the service. We may disclose information if required by law.

## Your choices

Because the app has no account, there is no stored profile to sign into or delete. You control what is sent by
choosing what to ask. For questions or requests about this policy, contact **support@pivota.ai**.

## Children

The app is intended for a general audience and is **not** directed to children under 13.
