# Bounded catalog alternatives and exact evidence hydration

The builder now hydrates exact listing evidence for its selected anchors and selected baseline candidates, then rescoring runs on those hydrated facts. This fixes the previous behavior where an older anchor/candidate could not receive ingredient or published product-intel evidence outside the newest bounded KB load. Existing selector SQL, uncovered ordering/cooldown, and the default broad source pool remain unchanged. Exact evidence selection is capped at 5,000 unique products, with every selected anchor first. `sourceDiagnostics.targeted_evidence` reports requested/loaded/omitted product counts and `selection_complete`; a cap hit does not mean evidence is complete. Ingredient formulas are never joined by name or family.

Full-catalog candidate expansion is a separate opt-in: `--expand-target-recall` on the build, routine, or sync wrapper, or `RELGRAPH_SYNC_EXPAND_TARGET_RECALL=true` for cron. All default cron/runtime flags remain off. The selected anchor slice is fixed before expansion. Extra products cannot enter the anchor lane or the affected need-node lane.

Each bounded anchor batch searches the full eligible catalog before target limits. It uses specific category/job hints and ranks same-job cross-brand opportunities with one winner per brand before taking further listings. Brands absent from the baseline pool receive an opportunity; catalog update time does not exclude older brands. Brand diversity never contributes to similarity or approval. Raw catalog targets get exact evidence hydration before scoring and stay scoped to the anchor that recalled them. Baseline related/complement candidates keep their lanes. Shared structural policy rejects mismatched application area, tool/topical form, set composition, and same-family variants. The existing scorer, inference, prefilter, review, fan-in, and serving gates still decide whether an alternative is usable. No pair similarity or dupe evidence is fabricated. Price comparisons continue to require one known shared currency.

Expansion requires a live sync row, active/observed eligible merchant source, a signed product, unsuppressed state, public `catalog_row_trust`, and a positive render probe computed between now minus seven days and now. It requires explicit market evidence from the catalog recall projection, product payload/snapshot, or an explicitly active seed belonging to this exact listing; unknown markets fail closed. A seed's attachment must match the catalog key. An unattached seed can bind by source ID only in the stable `platform='external_seed'` source lane or a global `ext_` identity. The lane survives observed seller rekeying; a seller's name or a native store's recycled ID cannot establish market. A known conflicting catalog/payload market cannot be overridden by a seed. Known unavailable rows are excluded. These are candidate-stage checks: they do not establish a verified fresh priced offer. Diagnostics explicitly report `verified_fresh_offer:false`; offer freshness and final serving readiness remain separate review/serving responsibilities.

| --- | ---: | ---: | --- |
| `--target-recall-max-anchors` | 200 | 400 | `RELGRAPH_SYNC_TARGET_RECALL_MAX_ANCHORS` |
| `--target-recall-batch-size` | 20 | 20 | `RELGRAPH_SYNC_TARGET_RECALL_BATCH_SIZE` |
| `--target-recall-per-anchor` | 64 | 96 | `RELGRAPH_SYNC_TARGET_RECALL_PER_ANCHOR` |
| `--target-recall-max-candidates` | 5000 | 5000 | `RELGRAPH_SYNC_TARGET_RECALL_MAX_CANDIDATES` |
| `--target-recall-max-pages` | 2 | 3 | `RELGRAPH_SYNC_TARGET_RECALL_MAX_PAGES` |

The aggregate target budget is distributed over eligible selected anchors. The builder reserves evidence capacity for anchors, so its actual target budget can be lower than the requested maximum. Target-recall query execution is sequential, at most 60 target-recall statements per build; default target-recall settings need at most 20. Exact evidence hydration is separate and also runs without expansion: up to 5,000 selected listings in batches of 200, with Insights and ingredient reads plus table checks in each batch. The 60-statement ceiling does not bound the entire build. Each page returns bounded rows, with total target output no greater than the aggregate budget and total raw reads no greater than budget times pages. SQL carries compact retrieval fields through the full catalog scan and fetches product payloads only for page winners. `sourceDiagnostics.target_recall` reports actual queries, rows, target counts, structural rejections, caps, and unqueried anchors. Missing catalog/trust support fails expansion closed; schema-column drift is an error. Remaining limits include absent explicit market/trust/render evidence, no useful category/job hint, catalog facts too sparse to prove pair utility, and capped pages that may omit compatible lower-ranked targets.

The catalog scan builds a deduplicated token dictionary once per eligible listing and uses key membership for each anchor's overlap count. Repeating a word cannot inflate evidence overlap. Trust membership is uncorrelated so a low eligibility estimate cannot trigger a full trust-table scan per product. These changes preserve the original unique-token relevance count and deterministic brand ranking.

Validation uses synthetic unit fixtures and a throwaway local Postgres database, without product detail exports, live model calls, or production writes. The PG suite covers old unseen brands against 2,000 newer house rows, strict eligibility exclusions, exact seed ownership, explicit-market fallback, incompatible structures, and batched multi-anchor bounds. Its deadline regression scans 24,000 eligible same-category products for 20 anchors with 300-word description/recall text and 24 anchor tokens, under a 30-second database statement timeout, returning 500 candidates. A local isolated `EXPLAIN ANALYZE` measured about 6.1 seconds after optimization, versus a 30-second timeout before it. This synthetic stress check is not a production latency guarantee; returned-row and query-count caps do not cap catalog size or the work of a full scan.
