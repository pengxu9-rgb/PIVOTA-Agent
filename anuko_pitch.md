## Three findings that land

**1. ChatGPT can send shoppers to your store — Gemini can't, and the brush is nowhere.**
When a shopper *names* the product, ChatGPT cites **anukoofficial.com** directly — on **38%** of "where to buy the bond-repair oil" queries, 25% for the hair butter — even flagging your *"direct purchase path."* But **Gemini cites your own site 0% of the time** for every SKU, and the **Mini Detangle Brush isn't cited first-party on either engine** (AI points to Carousell and rival brushes instead). One engine half-knows you; the other routes entirely around you.

**2. You don't own the category answer.**
Ask the buying question — "best bond-repair hair oil," "best deep-conditioning hair mask" — and AI names ANUKO 44–56% of the time (good demand signal!) but grounds the answer in **Olive Young and marketplaces**, not your page. You're a mention inside someone else's shelf, not the cited recommendation.

**3. Reach without proof — with receipts.**
DeepSeek verified the answers where AI *did* mention ANUKO and flagged three: the *"reviews"* and *"does it actually work"* answers **"only state availability… no reviews or recommendation support," "no evidence of effectiveness."** AI can confirm you're for sale. It has nothing to say about whether you work.
