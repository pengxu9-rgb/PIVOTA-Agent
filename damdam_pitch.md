## Three findings that land

**1. Indexed, not endorsed.**
AI can *find* DAMDAM — its own site, Sephora, eBay all show up. But **no independent source recommends it** when a shopper asks the category question. That's distribution, not endorsement.

**2. Competitors eat the answer.**
When buyers ask for alternatives, AI substitutes a rival for every hero product:

| DAMDAM product | Buyer asks… | AI names instead |
|---|---|---|
| Mochi Mochi Luminous Cream | "best cream" | **La Roche-Posay** |
| Citrus Glow Vit-C Serum | "what helps with summer" | **Murad** |
| Nomad's Cream Cleanser | "best face cleansers" | **Tata Harper** |
| Paradisi Mist Essence | "best hydrating essence" | **Beauty of Joseon** |

Most-named rivals overall: CeraVe, La Roche-Posay, Neutrogena, Laneige, The Ordinary, COSRX. **The demand is there — DAMDAM just isn't the pick.**

**3. Reach without trust.**
Even where AI *does* cite DAMDAM, DeepSeek flagged that "does it actually work?" answers only confirm the product is **for sale** — no efficacy evidence. Visibility without a reason to believe.

> For "best moisturizer" queries, AI routes buyers to **sephora.com, eBay, and Mercari — not DAMDAM's own site.**

## What Pivota does about it

1. Make each hero SKU's **official page the citable + buyable canonical answer** — grounded product evidence, vs-competitor comparisons, and direct-buy proof.

3. **Re-measure with this same audit** — before/after, SKU by SKU. Visibility becomes a number you can move.
